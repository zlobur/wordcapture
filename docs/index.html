How it works

1

Select a word

Highlight any word on a page. The Wc badge appears instantly.

2

See the translation

Click the badge. Translation, transcription, and CEFR level appear in a bubble.

3

Save to your collection

Hit Save. The word goes to your inbox. Move it to a deck when ready.

4

Review & learn

Study with spaced repetition. The algorithm schedules reviews at optimal intervals.

Privacy Policy

Data collected

Your saved words and settings are stored locally in your browser via Chrome storage API. Words you translate are sent to our backend for processing.

Not collected

No browsing history, no personal information, no analytics linked to you. We don't track which pages you visit.

Third parties

Translation and AI enrichment are handled by our own backend. Only the word you translate is sent — never the surrounding page content.

Storage

All vocabulary data is local to your browser. Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data.
